Asset type: front illustration source for a printable 5 × 3.5 inch landscape postcard.

Primary instruction: Transform the supplied Rouen Place du Vieux-Marché photograph into a polished vintage travel-poster illustration matching the supplied approved postcard illustrations. Preserve the recognizable Old Market Square composition: the long row of varied half-timbered medieval façades sweeping from the left toward the center, the prominent pink-and-cream gabled building on the right, the broad blue sky, café awnings at street level, and the low stone ruins across the foreground. Simplify or omit tiny modern signs, flags, people, bicycles, street furniture, and photographic clutter; do not introduce new landmarks.

Composition: exact 10:7 landscape ratio. Artwork fills the upper approximately 80% of the canvas. Reserve the lower approximately 20% as a completely blank, uninterrupted deep navy-teal title band, matching the reference cards. Keep all architecture and ruins above the band. No border.

Style: flat hand-pulled screen-print / mid-century travel poster; bold simplified shapes; crisp architectural silhouettes; limited palette of deep navy-teal, muted warm cream, dusty rose, brick red, ochre, pale blue, and restrained sage; subtle distressed ink and aged-paper texture; strong contrast; no gradients, no photorealism, no glossy effects.

Text: no text, no title, no subtitle, no logo, no watermark, and no lettering anywhere in the image. The bottom band must remain fully blank for later typography.
