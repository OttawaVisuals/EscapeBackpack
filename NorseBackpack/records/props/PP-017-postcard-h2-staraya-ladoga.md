**15 Oct 2026 (designer, envelope 3 text pass, PZ-004):** H2 now introduces Harald and gives a hint at DRINK. Still D, R, I, N, K:

> **D**inners take hours here and nobody rushes, which suits me fine. / **R**urik supposedly ruled from right here, if the old chronicles are true. / **I** climbed up on the fortress wall for a look at the whole river bend. / **N**estled in a cosy corner with a hot tea, I've been reading about the old saga and what our potential ancestors achieved. / **K**nowing that Harald Hardrada came through here as a teenage exile, long before he was king, makes my own trip feel short. / Love, Aunt Liv

Replaces “Nobody warned me how far this feels from anywhere else.” and “Keeping up with new puzzles for you is starting to be a challenge!” (the fourth-wall line from Q-057). “Next” is avoided on purpose so nobody reads a route order into it. Fun Fact: “A rune stick found here is one of Russia's oldest runic finds.” **Fact check:** Harald was about 15 at Stiklestad (1030) and fled east to Rus' (well attested); that he passed through Staraya Ladoga is only probable in the sources found (Wikipedia, vikingr.org), not established. Wording choice open: “came through here” vs “may have come through here”. PNG render checked; acrostic intact.

**15 Oct 2026 (designer):** K sentence softened to “Knowing that Harald Hardrada may have come through here as a teenage exile, long before he was king, makes my own trip feel short.” (the Staraya Ladoga stay is only probable in the sources). Rebuilt.

**15 Oct 2026 (designer, correction):** the designer meant the I sentence to be the Harald introduction, not the old river-bend line, and the K line to stay “Knowing how far Harald still had to go makes my own trip feel short.” The two notes above that give other I and K wording are superseded. Current H2: **D**inners take hours here and nobody rushes, which suits me fine. / **R**urik supposedly ruled from right here, if the old chronicles are true. / **I** climbed the fortress wall and thought of Harald Hardrada, who may have turned up here as a teenage exile before he became king of Norway. / **N**estled in a cosy corner with a hot tea, I've been reading about the old saga and what our potential ancestors achieved. / **K**nowing how far Harald still had to go makes my own trip feel short. / Love, Aunt Liv. “may have” was added by Claude to the I sentence, following the designer's earlier decision to soften the Staraya Ladoga claim; the designer's own wording was “who turned up here”. Rebuilt and rendered.
