**15 Oct 2026 (fact check of the new message):** checked against the web, mainly the Laxdæla saga text ([sagadb.org](https://sagadb.org/files/html/laxdaela_saga.en2.html), Proctor translation) and [Wikipedia on Aud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aud_the_Deep-Minded_(Ketilsd%C3%B3ttir)) and [on Björn the Easterner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bj%C7%ABrn_the_Easterner). **Holds:** Aud (Unn) sailed from Scotland to Breiðafjörður with kin, about twenty men and thralls; she freed the thralls and gave land to them and her crew; she claimed land in the Dalir (settling at Hvammur); she first met her brother Helgi, then went to her brother Björn, who took her in, and she wintered with him. Björn the Easterner lived at Bjarnarhöfn (the saga's “Bearhaven”), so “her brother's harbor” is right. **Wording notes:** “shipful of followers” is fair (about twenty men plus kin); “claimed land of her own” happened after the winter, in the Dalir, not at Bjarnarhöfn itself, so “sailed here … then claimed land” is loose but not wrong. Fun Fact: the shark is Greenland shark cured as hákarl, shown at the Bjarnarhöfn shark museum; not separately verified for “cured for months”. Not checked: the saga's claim that Bjarnarhöfn and Bearhaven are the same place beyond the translators' naming.
