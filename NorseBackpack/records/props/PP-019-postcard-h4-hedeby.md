**13 Oct 2026 (designer, after playtest 1):** on the 6 × 4 in card (PR-004) the rune key is about 1.15× and its box is shorter: "his key, copied out:" and the footnote sit to the right of the grid instead of above and below it, because the larger message leaves less height under the signature.

**14 Oct 2026 (designer, box + envelopes):** removed “From here I’m turning south: nothing but Mediterranean weather for the rest of this trip.” It was an ordering clue for the old finale; the marks give the order now (Q-054), and H4 arrives in envelope 11, after the southern cards (envelope 3). The message is now “Today: Hedeby — barely a Hedeby left.” plus the branch-rune line; the key moved up with it.

**17 Oct 2026 (designer, Q-057):** Fun Fact shortened to “Harald burned Hedeby to the ground, sailing burning ships into its harbour.” (the Denmark clause is gone; the history is not checked against a source). Message and the rune key table unchanged. H4 is in envelope 11. Rebuilt.

**17 Oct 2026 (designer):** message gains a third line so players link the runes to a map: “You just have to map the correct ones to get the final value.” (the designer typed it without the final full stop; a full stop was added to match the other lines). The table moved down to fit; it still sits above the footer. Rebuilt.
