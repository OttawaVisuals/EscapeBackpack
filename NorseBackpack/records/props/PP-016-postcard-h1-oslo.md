Style: flat hand-pulled screen-print / mid-century travel poster; bold simplified shapes; crisp architectural silhouettes; limited palette of deep midnight navy, muted cobalt blue, warm limestone cream, golden ochre light, restrained rust, dark teal, and subdued olive; subtle distressed ink and aged-paper texture; strong contrast; no gradients, no photorealism, no glossy effects.
