The cross, bow, lightning bolt, tree, teacup, Roman numeral I, plain “to” arrow, fish-scale patch and five-pip die are built as a coherent multicolour transparent set and are explicitly Liv’s drawings, not publisher furniture.
**All nine built, per PZ-011:** cross + bow + lightning bolt on Rollo’s Bayeux card (stop 3); tree + teacup on his Winchester card (stop 4); fish-scales + Roman numeral I + plain arrow + die-5 on his Battle card (stop 5), reading **SCALE 1 TO 5** (target changed to 1:125 on 28 Sept 2026; drawing still to redo, PZ-011). Bayeux is the card that establishes why the drawings start appearing at all.
**Dropped, 26 Sept 2026:** the tenth icon, the arrow/2 hybrid, had no card assignment and no confirmed puzzle to belong to — removed from the set rather than left unused.
