The Varangian Guard were the emperor’s axe-bearing guard and carried the **Dane axe** — a single broad blade on a long two-handed haft. A double-headed labrys is Minoan. **Replacement artwork built:** `Postcards/Stamps/Stamp_Harald_Dane_Axe_v1.png`; all seven Harald card builders now use it.
