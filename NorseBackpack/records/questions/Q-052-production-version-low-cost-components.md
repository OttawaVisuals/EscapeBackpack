Opened 11 Oct 2026. The designer is weighing a Kickstarter run now that the box format (PR-007) has cut costs. Ideas from the designer, with what each one has to keep working. All candidates, none decided.

| Component | Designer's idea | What it must still do | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aud's comb (PP-028) | Cardboard cut | Sit over AD's message as a grille (PZ-003) | A die-cut card grille can be more precise than a real comb. |
| Treasure tally (PP-026) | Cardboard, maybe | Hold the coin total and read SOLE when turned upside down (3705) | Possible as a card strip with four wheels in windows, the same die-cut and rivet process as the cypher wheel (PP-047). Digits must stay strict seven-segment so 7 reads as L. |
| Trail maps (PP-029/030/032/034) | Not laminated | Be traced by eye or finger (PZ-013) | Lamination was for the wet-erase marker, which is parked (PP-042). Unlaminated maps can also be folded in half, which fixes the envelope fit in Q-049 (a folded letter sheet fits a 6 × 9 in envelope). Harald's map is printed on both sides; check the back still reads. |
| Hnefatafl (PP-035) | Cardboard board; stock meeples or pieces | 11 × 11 board, 16 dark, 6 light and a king that looks different | Stock wooden pawns in two colours plus one taller piece for the king. The board's printed size isn't recorded; it must fit the box (fold it if needed). |
| Magnifier (PP-027) | Something cheap, like a ~$1 Lego magnifying glass | Make the small branch runes (PZ-012) and the 1576 top edge (PZ-002) readable | Claude's doubt, not checked: the Lego piece is a minifigure accessory and may not magnify at all. A flat Fresnel card magnifier (credit-card size) does magnify, is cheap in bulk and fits envelope 1. Needs a test on the printed runes. |

**Not raised yet:** the architect's scale ruler (PP-025). A printed card ruler with the needed scales would cost cents and fit an envelope if it is under the box's inside length (Q-049). Also coins (PP-037) and the medallion (PP-044).

Prices above are not quoted; "cheap" means "likely, to be checked".

**Parts list, 11 Oct 2026:** `Production/Norse_Parts_List.xlsx` (built once by `Production/build_parts_list.py`, which won't overwrite it) lists every component per box with these production ideas and blank price columns at 500 and 1,000 units. Prices typed there don't flow back into the records.
