**17 Oct 2026 (designer):** envelopes: ImprintNow storage envelopes (imprintnow.ca/product/envelopes/storage-envelopes) look like "a good site for cheap but printed string envelopes". Idea: buy them in bulk with the adventure logo, then stamp the cypher on each one ourselves. Read from the page 17 Oct: only 15 × 22 cm is listed (9 × 12 in would be a custom-size quote); imprinted, CAD $1.51 at 300, $1.35 at 500, $1.30 at 1,000, $1.01 at 5,000; foil +35 %, front and back +15 %. Not checked: that these are string-and-button, and whether one order can hold several versions (logo plus a different rune label per envelope) priced on the total quantity. Every copy of an adventure uses the same labels, so one self-inking stamp per envelope label (not one per rune) would do: about 15 stamps per adventure.

**17 Oct 2026, plain string-tie envelopes (web search, listing prices, not quoted):** in North American office supply shops, most 9 × 12 in string-and-button envelopes are interoffice ones with a printed routing grid or name lines (Quality Park #90, about US $0.61–0.72 each per 100; Blue Summit, about $0.39 each per 100), so they don't suit the game. Plain ones are rare and sold in small packs (JAM Paper, 25 per pack). Uline's string-and-button envelope is 10 × 13 in only (about $0.37–0.40 each), which is too long for the box. Chinese manufacturers on Alibaba make custom-logo kraft string-and-button envelopes at about US $0.03–0.35 each, with minimums around 1,000 (one supplier: $0.21 each at 5,000+). Freight is extra, and a factory could print each rune label as its own version. Their standard A4/C4 size is 229 × 324 mm (12¾ in long), longer than the box's 12⅛ in inside, so it would need a custom 9 × 12 in size (an Alibaba guide says custom sizes add 15–30 %).
