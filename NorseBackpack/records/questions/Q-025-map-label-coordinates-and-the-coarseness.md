Status on the old page: **Accepted as-is, 29 Sept 2026**

The printable maps are built by `NorseBackpack/TravelMap/build_trail_maps_pdf.py`. Most settlement labels are drawn from `stops.js`, the 72-entry sourced research corpus, so their coordinates are already part of the project’s vetted dataset. The remainder are curated extras marked `APPROX` in the config: typed from general knowledge, good to roughly a tenth of a degree, **not source-checked**. **Why this is not merely tidy-up:** two Icelandic names in the first draft — “Reyðhol” and “Hvammsýla” — were **not real places**. They came from faulty recall and were caught only on visual inspection. Reykholt was the real place being garbled. Every `APPROX` label needs checking against a source before any sheet is printed for play. **Separately:** Natural Earth 50m is coarse at Aud’s 4.5° frame — Breiðafjörður’s fjords are smoothed and Snæfellsnes is blocky. Acceptable as a stylised poster map, not as a survey. Finer Natural Earth data would fix it if that ever matters. **Accepted as-is, 29 Sept 2026:** the user reviewed the maps and they are good enough; the `APPROX` labels are not being source-checked (in line with old page HI-05, dropped).
