Status on the old page: **Decided**

**Built 1 Oct 2026** in [Digital/Leif.html](../Digital/Leif.html) (locks 10–12). The Hnefatafl board is an 11 × 11 grid lettered A–K and numbered 1–11 like Liv’s sheet, corners and throne marked, with a tray of 16 dark pieces, 6 light pieces and the king below it; pieces are dragged onto squares, no rules are enforced, and “Clear the board” puts them all back. The setup comes from the back of Harald’s map (the separate board-setup insert is not used online). The museum ticket gets a third face, “held to the light”: the front with the mirrored back showing through the right way round, so columns I–K can be read without a lamp (`Digital/build_harald_digital_assets.py`). The board is drawn 5 in square (estimate) plus its tray.
