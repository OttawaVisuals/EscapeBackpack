Status on the old page: **Open**

**Built 2 Oct 2026** by Claude Design in [Digital/Leif.html](../Digital/Leif.html), without a proposal first; reviewed and four fixes made by Claude Code the same day. Added: the game is titled *The Raven Inheritance*; an opening with Liv’s handwritten letter (Caveat font, SIL OFL, `Digital/assets/`); a padlock with turning wheels (digits or letters, typed or dragged); a lock-open banner that asks for a 1–5 rating; a list of codes tried per lock; notes pinned to keepsakes; table zoom and tablet gestures; a drawn backpack whose pockets match the locks; an ending with play time, hints and tries, a result to copy, a printable certificate and a feedback form (the site’s Formspree form); synthesised sound (`leif-sound.js`, effects on, ambience off); optional analytics through Zaraz if present; and “Play together” rooms (`leif-room.js` and the `escapepack-rooms` Cloudflare Worker), hidden until the Worker is deployed and its address is set in the page. Lock 6 now opens with 104 (PZ-011). **Fixed in review:** the sorting lanes lay under the props on the final route; they now sit in their own band below the 52 × 28 in table, which grows to 52 × 44.4 in while they are out or while a card still lies in them. The How to play text said the game never moves your keepsakes; it now names Tidy and the piles at a new trail (Q-042). **Decided 2 Oct 2026:** Claude Design’s opening letter repeated postcard L1 (heritage, the four Vikings, where to start), so the opening now quotes L1 word for word: “Hello nephew! I know you love puzzles, so I created an adventure for you! With a surprise at the end!”, signed “With love, Aunt Liv”. **Decided 2 Oct 2026:** no note from Liv between trails (Claude Design’s four notes repeated the postcards and the nudges, and the last one steered the final riddle). **Decided later the same day:** nothing on screen distinguishes the trails, because which cards belong together is part of the final riddle (see Q-005). The masthead reads “LOCK 7 OF 13” (“THE FINAL ROUTE” for the last lock); the trail row under the backpack is gone; the keepsakes shelf and the pinned-notes list group by kind (Postcards, Maps, Tickets & papers, Objects); a pop-up card appears only for the final route and the end; the sorting lanes are removed; piles follow age (Q-042). Props take the titles printed on them: The Western Lands, The Granted Lands, The Island Settlement, The Varangian Road, Carved comb; the pocket “Rollo’s pouch” is the “Small pouch”; nudges, messages and hints use those titles. Kept: “Aud’s Treasure Museum ticket” (printed on the ticket) and “Harald’s horn” in lock 11 (the puzzle’s subject). The leftover “Leif’s trail” wording (restart dialog, save file name, lock 3’s message) now speaks of the whole game. **Published 2 Oct 2026** at [escapepack.ca/play/norse/leif/](https://escapepack.ca/play/norse/leif/) (design repo `f830d6e`, site repo `a1d1d51`). **Padlock (2 Oct 2026):** each wheel has a ▲ (next) and ▼ (back) button; clicking the wheel face no longer turns it, because its top half went back while ↑ and drag-up went forward. **Backpack panel (3 Oct 2026):** on laptops the small bag sits beside the lock’s name and nudge, the “Turn the wheels to a four-digit combination” line is dropped, and spacing is tighter, so the padlock and notebook fit without scrolling at 1366×768. The line under each lock’s name only says what came out of the last lock; the hints that followed it (“Read them side by side”, “What was in Harald’s horn?”…) are dropped. **Top bar and hints (3 Oct 2026):** on laptops, save, load and restart are in a “Game” menu in the top bar next to a “Feedback” button; hints are four folds (Hint 1, 2, 3, Solution) that open and close independently, and the result counts the furthest one opened. **Flip and shortcuts (5 Oct 2026):** flipping a prop plays a short 3D turn (instant with reduced motion); buttons with a keyboard shortcut show the key as a small badge (M, R, F, ↵, Esc), hidden on touch screens. **Open:** (1) The lock chain still releases one trail at a time until the new order in Q-005 is set. (2) Play together: deploy the Worker or leave it hidden; `escapepack-rooms` is not yet a git repository. Minor: Ctrl+P at any time prints only the certificate (blank before the end); on a phone the table leaves large empty areas.
