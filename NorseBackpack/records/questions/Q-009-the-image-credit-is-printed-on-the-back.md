Status on the old page: **Decided · card L1 done**

**Original objection:** a file path on a prop that is meant to be a postcard from a gift shop. The proposed fix was to move attribution to the print-sheet margin outside the trim line, since the credits page plus a sheet-level credit satisfies the licence.
**Overtaken in part.** The credit now has a second job: it is the camouflage for the publisher’s imprint that carries the `F1` grid reference (old page PZ-10). Players skip that grey strip because it is visibly boring, and the imprint hides inside it. Strip the credit off the card and the imprint becomes the only small grey text on an otherwise clean layout — more conspicuous, not less. **Decided: the attribution stays on the card.The immersion point still stands, though**, and it is narrower than it first looked. What breaks the gift-shop illusion is not the credit — real postcards print photographer credits — it is the line `Source and full attribution: Postcards/Image_Credits.html`. A file path is the only anachronism on the card.
**Done on card L1.** The `Source and full attribution` file-path line is dropped; one credit line remains — *Image adaptation: “L’Anse aux Meadows, The Meeting of Two Worlds” — D. Gordon E. Robertson, CC BY-SA 3.0.* That carries the modification notice, title, author and licence, so it satisfies CC BY-SA 3.0 on its own; the source URL and adaptation licence stay on the credits page. The imprint sits right-aligned opposite it on the same baseline.
**Cards L2 and L3 are not done, and must not be** until Q-010 is fixed — their printed URLs are currently their only attribution record anywhere.
