**Built 1 Oct 2026** in [Digital/Leif.html](../Digital/Leif.html) (locks 7–9; lock 6 is shown but cannot open until PZ-011 is set). Following Q-038, the tally arrives with the coins at lock 7 rather than lying loose from lock 1. The tally has four 3D wheels (drag or scroll each one) and reads SOLE when turned upside down. The 12 coins arrive as one mixed pile, each simply called “Coin”, drawn like the ticket’s Coin Room key. The comb settles only face up, on AD’s message side, square to the card and with both holes near the printed dots; seated, it travels and turns with the card. Art: `Digital/build_aud_digital_assets.py`. When a new prop finds no free spot even on the largest table, the whole table is laid out again (with a message).
