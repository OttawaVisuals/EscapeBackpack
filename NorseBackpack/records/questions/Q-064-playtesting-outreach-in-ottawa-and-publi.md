**21 Oct 2026 (designer):** where to find local people to try the prototype. This is the "blind playtests" phase of `Production/Kickstarter_Plan.md` (phase 1, rounds B and C). Nobody has been contacted yet.

**For game testing:**

- **Ottawa Strategy Games** (Meetup: meetup.com/ottawa-strategy-games). Meets weekly, free. Idea: ask the organizer for a slot at a Monday night or after a "Learn to Play" session.
- **Studio Staja** (https://staja.ca/). Small board game café that organizes events.
- **Level One** (https://www.levelonegamepub.com/hiw). The largest board game café in Ottawa.
- **ProtoTO.** Toronto tabletop game design convention (travel needed; not checked whether it runs this season).
- **Reddit post.** Draft below; post in the Ottawa subreddit first.

**Potential publisher:** Black Suit Games (https://www.blacksuitgames.com/). Not contacted. Look at what they publish before reaching out.
