Opened 14 Oct 2026 (designer). L1 says Liv has been exploring “our Norse heritage”, but nothing in the game reveals how the family is connected. The designer suggests a reveal that the family descends from one of the four figures (Leif, Rollo, Aud, Harald). Today the ending is the plane ticket and Liv's invitation letter (PP-043, not yet written) plus the medallion (PP-044).

Options raised in chat (none chosen):

1. **Leif.** The family name already points there: the transition tickets name the passenger ERICSON / LIV, and the nephew was “John Ericson” on the old address lines (`Props/Tickets/build_transition_tickets_pdf.py`). Leif is also the first trail. Historically his descendants are not documented, so this is pure fiction.
2. **Rollo.** The most plausible in real life: the Norman dukes and English kings descend from him, and the Rollo trail already follows his descendants (ST-003).
3. **Aud.** Many Icelanders trace back to her through the settlement sagas.
4. **Left to the end:** the crest or medallion shows which one, so the reveal is the payoff of the final puzzle.

Also open: **the greeting.** L1 says “Hello nephew!”. The designer asked about “nibling” to be more inclusive. Claude's view: many players, especially children, won't know the word, and it reads oddly in Liv's voice. Alternatives: a greeting that needs no relationship word (“Hello, my dear explorer!”), or “Dear niece or nephew”. Not decided.

**14 Oct 2026 (designer): answered.** The family descends from **Aud the Deep-Minded** (option 3). Greeting: “Hello, my dear explorer!” (replaces “Hello nephew!”). Not yet decided: where and how the reveal is made (Liv's final letter PP-043 is the obvious place; whether the crest or medallion also shows it), and whether the ERICSON surname on the transition tickets should change, since it now points at Leif rather than Aud.
