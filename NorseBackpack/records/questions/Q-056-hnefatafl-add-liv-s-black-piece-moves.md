Opened 14 Oct 2026 (designer, to do later). The hnefatafl puzzle (PZ-008) has players move only the king: three straight-line moves from the throne to a corner, counting each leg (`253`). The designer wants the black (attacking) pieces to move too, to make the puzzle better. **Liv defines the black moves**: the player plays the king, and Liv's papers say how black answers each move.

Open: where Liv's black moves are written (H1, the Oslo ticket, the map's back, a new note), whether the answer stays `253` or changes (a new code means rebuilding the wheel labels, PP-047), and whether the black moves should block wrong king routes so only one three-move escape works. Not started.

**17 Oct 2026 (designer):** direction taken, design in PZ-008: black plays 4 moves (E1-E6, H2-H5, H10-J10, J10-J11), the king escapes in 4 moves, answer 2233; two of the black moves are shown as arrows on the back of Harald's map, a cryptic note gives their order, and a puzzle on a place of Harald's map gives the last black position. Still open: the exact arrows, the note and the last-move puzzle, then the build. Left open until built.

**17 Oct 2026 (designer):** design now **2134** (replaces the 2233 note above): black E1-E6, H2-H5, H10-E10, I9-I11; king F6-H6, H6-H7, H7-K7, K7-K11; layout with I8 and I9 added (18 dark). Details and the build list are in PZ-008. Still open: the arrows, the order note and how moves 3 and 4 are given.

**17 Oct 2026 (designer):** final version in PZ-008: 19 dark (K10 added), black E1-E6, H2-H5, H10-H11, K10-I10 (the last move opens the escape), king F6-H6-H7-K7-K11 = 2134. Move 3 riddle: the attacker on the second-highest row steps up to the top row. Move 4: the shield icon at I10 on Harald's map (to move from I11) and on the ticket. Still open until built.
