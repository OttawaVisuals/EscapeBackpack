Casting the *öndvegissúlur* overboard is Ingólfr Arnarson’s, not Aud’s. Her documented motif is **the comb she lost at Kambsnes**, which Landnámabók says the headland is named for — and which ties the stamp to the comb prop and to `KAMBR`. **Replacement artwork built:** `Postcards/Stamps/Stamp_Aud_Comb_v1.png`; all four Aud card builders now use it.
