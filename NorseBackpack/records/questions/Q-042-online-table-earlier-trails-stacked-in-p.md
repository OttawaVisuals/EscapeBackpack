**Partly superseded 2 Oct 2026 (Q-043):** piles now follow age, not trails: Tidy piles everything older than the last two locks’ keepsakes, nothing is stacked when a lock opens, and the last lock brings piled postcards and maps back out. The text below is the original decision. **Built 2 Oct 2026** in [Digital/Leif.html](../Digital/Leif.html) at the user’s request, to keep the table tidy and small. Tidy table, and the lock that starts a new trail (locks 3, 6, 9 and 12), put props from finished trails into three fanned piles (postcards, maps, paper props) with each card’s name on the strip that shows; solid props (coins, tally, comb, ruler, board) go to the shelf. The current trail’s props and whatever the last lock released stay out; on the final route every postcard and map stays out, since lock 13 needs them all. A prop belongs to the trail under way when it arrives (Leif’s last lock hands over Rollo’s first props). Click a name, or a shelf entry, to take a card out to a free spot; dragging it off also works, and the pile closes up. Tidy then picks the smallest table that fits: trails now start on the 29 in table (Rollo, Aud) or 37 in (Harald) instead of 37–44 in. Marker lines, tally digits and board pieces are kept.
