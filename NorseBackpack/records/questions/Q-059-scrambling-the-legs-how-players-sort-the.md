Opened 15 Oct 2026 (designer, while reordering envelopes 1 to 3).

The designer wants the four legs (Leif, Rollo, Aud, Harald) mixed across the envelopes more than they are now, so that players have to sort the cards back into legs later. Q-005 already mixes the legs lock by lock (Direction of 2 Oct 2026); this asks for more, and for a reason to regroup.

**Open:** (1) how much mixing: every envelope, or only the early ones, with legs going clean near the end; (2) what lets players tell a card's leg apart and put it back (stamp motif, sender line, map, route marks: not checked yet; see Q-054 and the final puzzle PZ-013); (3) whether sorting into legs should itself feed the final puzzle (tracing routes on maps).

**Done so far:** envelopes 1 to 3 reordered (PR-007): AD + comb first, then L2 + L3 + magnifier, then H2 + H3 + HD, so the first three envelopes already cover Aud, Leif and Harald.
