Status on the old page: **Main image chosen**

**The profile raven inside the circular Norse knotwork frame is the current main image** and may become the family symbol. The spread-wing study remains a secondary option, not the lead. A matching frame-free profile raven has been derived for icon use; it preserves the same engraved bird and palette. The five earlier chick-like SVG ravens remain rejected.
