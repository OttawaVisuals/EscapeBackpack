Opened 11 Oct 2026, split from Q-046 once the box was set at **11.5 × 11.5 × 2 in** with all envelopes loose.

**The trail maps are the tight fit.** The four maps are letter sheets (8.5 × 11 in) laminated in gloss pouches (PZ-013). A standard letter laminating pouch is about 9 × 11.5 in, which is the box's outside size, and the box walls make the inside smaller. An envelope big enough for a laminated map (the usual one for letter sheets is 9 × 12 in) is longer than the box. Maps arrive in envelopes 4 (Aud), 6 (Leif), 8 (Rollo) and 10 (Harald). Options:

1. Trim each laminated map to just outside the paper (about 8.75 × 11.25 in) and find an envelope under the box's inside length.
2. Laminate on a smaller margin, or print the maps slightly smaller than letter (the map scales are printed on them, so a uniform shrink keeps distances right but every ruler reading changes, see Q-024/Q-044).
3. Put the four maps flat at the bottom of the box under a sealed card band per trail, instead of in envelopes.

Not measured: the box's inside size.

**Not flat, needs a padded or gusseted envelope:**

- Envelope 2: magnifier (PP-027; candidate is 90 mm with a wooden handle)
- Envelope 1: Aud's comb (PP-028)
- Envelope 9: architect's scale ruler (PP-025; a 30 cm ruler is about 11.8 in, longer than the box)
- Envelope 7: coin hoard (PP-037) and the four-wheel treasure tally (PP-026; size not recorded)
- Envelope 10: hnefatafl board and 23 pieces (PP-035)
- Envelope 13: adventure medallion (PP-044)

**The ruler does not fit lying flat** if it is the 30 cm one: the digital game models a "triangular 30 cm architect's scale ruler" (`Digital/leif-data.js`); the physical one on hand (PP-045, "Already at home") has no recorded length. A 30 cm ruler fits only along the diagonal (about 16 in inside, minus the walls), where it can't sit in an envelope. A 15 cm (6 in) ruler would fit, if the map scales still read on it.

**Depth:** 2 in for 13 envelopes plus the bulky items. Probably fine; not checked until the tally and pieces are sized.
