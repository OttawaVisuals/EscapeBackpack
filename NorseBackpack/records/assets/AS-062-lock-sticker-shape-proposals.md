8 October 2026: designer requested up to six alternatives to Claude's shield, each showing 6. Four proposals generated. **Designer selected C, the longship**, and requested all numbers in the existing Word sheet. PP-046 and AS-063 record the production update; shield print artwork is superseded.

Palette follows the existing icon set: pine green, rust and cream. Design target uses the existing 13 mm sticker size as a provisional assumption. These are enlarged raster concepts on white, not transparent cutouts or production print sheets. All four show the correct 6 and were visually inspected. Actual-size print readability and two-digit fit remain untested. Some generated shading remains despite the flat-colour prompt.

Earlier recommendation (superseded by the designer's selection of C): Codex recommended D for its compact silhouette and Norse character. C is the most recognizable narrative motif. A offers a simple broad number field; B's handle reduces the usable number area at equal overall height.
