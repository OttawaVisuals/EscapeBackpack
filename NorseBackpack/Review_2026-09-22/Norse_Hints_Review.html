The Raven Inheritance

A little help from Liv

Review playtest edition · optional hints

Optional hints for the proposed review version. Open one hint at a time. Solutions remain hidden until selected.

G1 · Opening

Hint 1

Match the postcard’s place to one luggage tag.

Hint 2

Use the two street numbers, not the names.

Hint 3

L’Anse aux Meadows goes first; Rouen goes second.

Show solution

1021: 10, then 21.

G2 · Beasts

Hint 1

Look for a small publisher’s imprint on the Greenland postcard.

Hint 2

Series F, No. 1 points to square F1 on the sea chart.

Hint 3

Identify that animal, then look up its letters on the back of the museum ticket.

Show solution

BEAR: B=3, E=2, A=1, R=2. Code 3212.

G3 · Joined skies

Hint 1

The cloud fragments on the two northern pictures belong together.

Hint 2

Try picture sides facing each other, one turned half a turn.

Hint 3

Align the top edges against a bright light and read along the join.

Show solution

The intended reading is 1576. If a finished print cannot show it, that is a prop fault, not a missed deduction.

G4 · Rouen word journeys

Hint 1

Four matching word-pairs from the ticket appear in the Rouen message. Either member of a pair counts.

Hint 2

Use fight/combat, people/folk, poultry/hen and inn/tavern in that order. The ticket supplies I6, F7, A3 and B9.

Hint 3

Find each matching map picture. Count grid steps from its square to its target; exclude the starting square.

Show solution

H6→I6 = 1; F11→F7 = 4; I3→A3 = 8; H9→B9 = 6. Code 1486.

G5 · Picture counts

Hint 1

Use the Chalus, Roumare Forest and Walcheren cards. Each message names something drawn on its front.

Hint 2

Count bolts, boars and boats. The Roumare message asks you to double the boar count.

Hint 3

Order the three by the historical periods in the Fun Facts: before 911, 911, then 1199.

Show solution

Five boats, three boars doubled to six, two bolts. Code 562. On Chalus, inspect the near round tower and the top of the tall tower.

G6 · Aud’s route

Hint 1

The ticket is a route, not a list of separate destinations. Start with the chapel-to-chapel leg already filled in.

Hint 2

Keep a separate tally for bridges, fords and gates. Count each crossing you traverse, not every symbol visible nearby.

Hint 3

At the standing stone, compare the village choices with A2’s forest observation. The intended route goes south toward H9, then on to the ruin and the cave.

Show solution

Intended totals: 5 bridges, 2 fords, 1 gate. Code 521. Designer must first verify this on the printed route.

G7 · Balance

Hint 1

Sort the coins. The cache’s mark selects the pile to weigh.

Hint 2

Use grams and zero the empty scale before putting the whole selected pile on it.

Hint 3

Drop the last decimal digit. Ignore the point, then read the remaining digits upside down.

Show solution

The intended 370.56 g truncates to 370.5; 3705 upside down reads SOLE. Do not use this gate until its cache mark and repeatable weight are physically verified.

G8 · Branch runes

Hint 1

The underlined word on the Sicily postcard matters to the map’s unusual column labels.

Hint 2

Use the columns R, A, V, E, N in that order. The Hedeby card is the decoding key.

Hint 3

For each selected stem, count its left branches for the key’s row and its right branches for the position in that row.

Show solution

Corrected map: R6→H, A10→R, V9→A, E3→F, N2→N. HRAFN. The original map also has a competing stem at E7; use the review correction.

G9 · The drink

Hint 1

Three postcards contribute three ideas: Kyiv, Staraya Ladoga and Constantinople.

Hint 2

Solve Kyiv’s riddle; look at the first letters of Ladoga’s five sentences; interpret sugar, bubbles and time.

Hint 3

You need a four-letter drink made from fermented honey.

Show solution

HONEY + DRINK + FERMENTED leads to MEAD. Fermentation or fermenting is a valid intermediate interpretation.

G10 · King’s escape

Hint 1

The map-back stain hides three columns. The museum ticket holds their mirrored impression.

Hint 2

Hold the ticket to light and align the grid to recover the missing pieces. Only the king moves; all others are obstacles.

Hint 3

Work backward from a corner. Try moving the king from F6 toward H6.

Show solution

F6 → H6 → H11 → K11. Distances 2, 5, 3 give 253. No captures, jumps or moving blockers.

G11 · Whole journey

Hint 1

Use stamps to form four groups, then compare the small marks with the journal’s family drawings.

Hint 2

Leave horned-helmet and double-axe cards out of the drawn routes. Keep those cards nearby to interpret ticket endpoints.

Hint 3

Use the first origin and last destination of each travel ticket to chain the four journeys. Journal and card clues order the places within each. Join place dots with straight lines, north up.

Show solution

Real orders: L1–L2–L3; R1–R2–R3–R4–R5–R6; A1–A2–A3; H1–H2–H3–H4–H5–H6. Intended shapes 1,9,7,2 in Leif–Rollo–Aud–Harald order: 1972. Physical digit recognition remains to be tested.

Optional discoveries

Rollo’s rebus cards

Bayeux: CROSS + BOW + BOLT. Winchester: TREE + TEA → TREATY. Battle: SCALE + I + TO + FIVE. These identify two map icons and a scale. The review version assigns no distance code; this is an optional discovery.

The comb

The intended idea is an overlay on the Bjarnarhofn card. Its extraction is not finished in this prototype. It is not required to enter Harald’s stage.
