    Places to choose

    Historical sources, map accuracy & saving

    This is a researched working catalogue of identifiable places associated with all four lives, including disputed traditions and contextual sites. No complete, verified travel log survives for any of them. Unnamed towns and unlocatable places are not invented as pins. The catalogue is not a claim to list every journey or every modern memorial.

    Supported means the connection is supported by the cited historical or heritage source; it does not mean an exact visit location is archaeologically proven. Saga means a later narrative reports it. Uncertain marks disputed identity, geography or association. Context is a research destination, not a claimed visit.

    Coordinates are approximate modern map anchors. Regional pins stand for areas, not exact landfalls, homes or battle positions. Historical regions do not match modern borders. Each place has its own source. Catalogue order is for browsing, not a verified chronology. Your selections always describe the aunt’s fictional itinerary.

    Plans are saved only in this browser, on this device and site address. Export a plan to keep a portable copy; import restores all four trips. No account or shared storage is used. Changing between a local file, local server and hosted site may use different browser storage.

    The bundled coast map works offline. Optional street detail needs the internet and uses OpenStreetMap. Tiny islands and coastline detail may be simplified on the offline map. Map projection: Web Mercator; north-up previews use the same projection.

    Map data: Natural Earth, public domain (1:50m land). Street detail: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Mapping: Leaflet · license.
