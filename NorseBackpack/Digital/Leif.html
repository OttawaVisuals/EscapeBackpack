    Leif’s trail

    Aunt Liv has sent you a backpack filled with keepsakes from her travels. She’s left you a postcard, two luggage tags and a museum ticket. The bag itself is locked.

    Start with her postcard. Read both sides and see where it leads.

    • Hover an object for its tools: Zoom for a readable close-up, Rotate, Flip and Move.
    • Use the magnifying glass (or press M) for small print. Scroll over the lens to change its strength.
    • Arrange objects as you like. Edges that come close snap together; hold Alt while dragging to place freely.
    • Enter combinations at the backpack. Hints are optional.

    Progress saves in this browser when storage is available. “Save a copy” lets you keep a backup or move devices. Best played on a laptop or desktop.
