The Raven Inheritance

A battered backpack has arrived from Aunt Liv, with a postcard, two luggage tags and a museum ticket tucked under its straps. Every pocket is locked.

Played best on a laptop, desktop or tablet, alone or together. Take your time: progress saves as you go.

How to play

Liv’s keepsakes lie on the table at real size. Read them, turn them over and put them together. When you find a combination, turn the padlock’s wheels beside the backpack and press Open. Each lock opens a pocket and brings out more keepsakes.

Mouse

  • Drag to move. Edges snap together; hold Alt to place freely.
  • Hover for Zoom, Rotate, Flip and Move.
  • Double-click for a readable close-up.
  • Ctrl + scroll, or + / −, zooms the table; drag the empty table to pan.

Tablet

  • Tap a keepsake for its tools; double-tap for a close-up.
  • Pinch the table to zoom, drag the empty table to pan.
  • Hold a keepsake and twist with a second finger to turn it.

Keyboard

  • M magnifier (scroll over the lens for strength)
  • R rotate, F flip, Enter close-up
  • + − 0 zoom, arrows move a keepsake
  • On the padlock: type the code, or ↑ ↓

Hints are optional and come one at a time. New keepsakes never push yours aside. Two things do move them: Tidy table, and the start of a new trail, when the finished trail’s keepsakes are stacked in piles (click a pile’s name to take one out). Progress saves in this browser; “Save a copy” keeps a backup or moves it to another device.
