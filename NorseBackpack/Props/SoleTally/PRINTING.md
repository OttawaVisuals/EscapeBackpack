# SOLE treasure tally — print notes

A four-wheel number counter for an escape-game prop. Players set the wheels to a
four-digit total; each wheel clicks between its ten digits.

## What to print

| File | Quantity |
|---|---|
| `SOLE_ring_body.stl` | 4 |
| `SOLE_base.stl` | 1 |
| `SOLE_endcap.stl` | 1 |

Parts are already oriented to print flat as exported.

## Settings (from the original model's notes)

- Rings: 100% infill. Base and end cap: 25% infill.
- Layer height 0.2 mm; 0.1–0.15 mm gives crisper digits if time allows.
- Single colour is fine. No supports expected — please check the slicer preview.

## After printing

- The digits are recessed 0.55 mm. Fill them with dark acrylic paint or wax crayon,
  then wipe the face clean. The groove on the base/end cap (reading line) can be filled the same way.
- The rings may feel stiff at first; turn each one through 10–20 full turns.

Mechanism: "4-digit counter" by BMdesign on Printables. Only the digits on the ring were redrawn.
