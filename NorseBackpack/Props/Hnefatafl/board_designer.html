Hnefatafl Board Designer

11×11 sandbox for finding the King's Escape puzzle's final layout (Escape Backpack, Norse project). Starting position below is an adapted tafl-style opening (inspired by traditional 8-defender ring patterns like Tablut's), not a verified historical ruleset for this exact board size — free to rearrange however you like. Move pieces by sliding (click a piece, then a highlighted destination); captures happen automatically when you sandwich an enemy piece between two of your own. This is a design sandbox, not a rules-enforced game — nothing stops illegal-looking positions if you use Place/Remove mode.
