Hnefatafl Board Designer

11×11 board for the King's Escape puzzle (Escape Backpack, Norse project). It starts on the real puzzle layout (16 dark, 6 light, king on F6, the same as board_layout.py). Slide a piece by clicking it, then a highlighted square, or type a move such as A8-D8. Turn on Puzzle run to play black → king, repeated (you choose how many king moves, 3 or 4, and how many black moves) and see the king's leg lengths and the code. Captures happen automatically (a piece sandwiched between two enemies). Use Place / edit to change the position.
