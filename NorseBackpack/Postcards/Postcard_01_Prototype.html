If this reached you, the field bag did too. I have spent years following our family story across the North Atlantic.

Open the bag, keep every postcard you find, and do not throw away the dates or place names.

For the first lock, the picture matters more than this message. Three travellers’ marks are hiding in the harbor. Read the view from left to right.

— Aunt Liv
