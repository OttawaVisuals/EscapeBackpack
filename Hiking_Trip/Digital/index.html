    DG-H21 — 2026-10-05 (Claude Code): 3D playable prototype of the flat Lego puzzle, built from the user’s Stud.io exports (prototypes/lego-flat/, one LDraw file per solution step). Not linked from the game. The user chose an interactive version over a hint animation. Rules confirmed by the user: the green 1 × 4 tile is the pusher; every orange tile is held by a stud and moves at most one stud; the red round plate + tile has a 1 written on it and falls out the side once free (gravity in person, animated online). Solution: push from the right, then the front (right-hand column), then the left; the last push moves the red piece one stud, which pushes an orange tile, and the red piece drops out the front. Interaction: click the green tile to pick it up, hover a hole to light it and preview the tile, click to slide it in as far as it goes (at most 3 studs), click the tile to pull it out. A grid simulation (prototypes/lego-sim.js, tested in prototypes/lego-flat.test.mjs) confirms right → front → left is the only way out. Without the one-stud limit, pushing from the left first would pop an orange tile out of the right side and free the red piece in one move. Pages: prototypes/lego-flat-play.html (play) and prototypes/lego-flat-3d.html (step-by-step solution).

    DG-H23 — 2026-10-05 (Claude Code, rules from the user): the blue square Lego puzzle is 3D too, built from the user’s exports (assets/lego-square-step0–4.ldr). The red 1 × 4 tile goes in from the back into the slot under the bridge (trans-red 1 × 2 brick + white 1 × 4 tile + blue 1 × 1 brick). The tile and the bridge drag each other: physically the player pushes the bridge, which drags the tile, but sliding the tile also drags the bridge. Every loose piece (bridge, two orange tiles) moves at most one stud and may stick out of the sides. Exported solution: tile in from the back; slide it left (the bridge goes one stud left); push it one stud deeper (an orange tile goes out the front); slide it right (the bridge returns and the other orange tile slides out the right side). The 5 is written on the end of that last orange tile, so the puzzle is solved when it is out. Shortest solution in the simulation: push the bridge left by hand, tile in from the back (it goes three studs deep), slide it right. Controls added for this puzzle: ◀ Slide / Push in / Slide ▶ while the tile is in (◀ ▶ follow the screen), and clicking the side of the bridge pushes it into that face. Code: lego-square.js; shared view lego-puzzle.js. Saves: push.square.actions and push.flat.actions (lists of moves); saves from the 2D versions start both puzzles afresh.
